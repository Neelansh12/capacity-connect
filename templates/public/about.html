About Capacity Connect

Digital Capacity Building & Learning Management Portal for MoES

1. Problem Context & Mission

Under the Smart India Hackathon 2026 theme of Smart Education & Administration, this portal addresses problem statement SIH26075 for the Ministry of Earth Sciences (MoES). Central scientific bodies require a dedicated, secure, and centralized organizational platform for employee training, transparent trainer discovery, continuous skill tracking, and verified institutional certification.

2. Architectural Decisions & Technology Stack

Capacity Connect is architected strictly as an enterprise-grade Django monolithic application using server-side rendering (SSR). It operates using:

  • Backend: Python, Django 5.x ORM, native sessions, form security, and role-based authorization.
  • Database: PostgreSQL with relational integrity, unique constraints, and automated Django migrations.
  • Frontend: Django Templates, compiled Tailwind CSS, TypeScript for interactivity, Chart.js for data analytics, and Lucide icons.
  • Document Generation: ReportLab for automated verifiable PDF certificates and downloadable executive audit reports.

3. Transparent Rule-Based Recommendation Engine

Unlike opaque black-box classifiers, Capacity Connect employs a fully explainable mathematical scoring formula to rank trainers against organizational competency requirements. Scores account for exact competency matches, instructor proficiency weights (Expert to Beginner), documented domain experience, and historical course completion records.
